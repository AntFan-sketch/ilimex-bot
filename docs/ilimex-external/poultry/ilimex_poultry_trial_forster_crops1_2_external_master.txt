Title: Ilimex Poultry Trial – A.J. Forster Farm (Three-Crop External Master)
Document Type: Trial Summary (External – Core)
Sector: Poultry
Use: External Bot
Last Updated: August 2026

Overview:
Ilimex conducted a controlled side-by-side commercial poultry comparison at A.J. Forster's farm, comparing House 2 fitted with the external Ilimex Flufence system with House 5 as the control.

The current approved external evidence covers three commercial crops. The results show a repeated mortality and commercial-performance signal under working-farm conditions. These are trial outcomes and must not be presented as guaranteed results for every farm.

Trial Design:
- House 2: Ilimex Flufence installed
- House 5: Control
- Approximately 21,500-22,000 birds per house
- Environmental monitoring via Hotraco Fortica systems
- Parameters tracked included CO2, water intake, ventilation, pressure and temperature

Environmental Context:
The monitored environmental conditions were closely matched between the houses. This supports the side-by-side comparison, but public wording should not overstate causality beyond the documented trial results.

Current Approved Results:
- Crop 1: approximately 0.5 percentage points lower mortality in the Ilimex house, equivalent to roughly 110 additional surviving birds
- Crop 2: approximately 0.5 percentage points lower mortality in the Ilimex house, again equivalent to roughly 110 additional surviving birds
- Crop 3: approximately 1.02 percentage points lower mortality, equivalent to 228 additional surviving birds
- Crop 3 was associated with an approximately 14% profit uplift in the trial comparison
- The farm subsequently confirmed an approximately 7% yield uplift from the external Ilimex unit

Commercial Interpretation:
Mortality, yield and profit are separate metrics. Do not imply that the full profit uplift resulted from mortality reduction alone, and do not merge the 7% yield uplift with the mortality or profit figures.

Approved External Wording:
"Across three commercial crops, the Ilimex house showed a repeated mortality and performance signal, including around 0.5 percentage points lower mortality in the first two crops and around 1.02 percentage points lower mortality in the third. Crop 3 was associated with an approximately 14% profit uplift in the trial comparison."

Restrictions:
- Do not guarantee the same result on another farm
- Do not claim that mortality, yield and profit are interchangeable measures
- Do not attribute the full commercial outcome to mortality alone
- Do not make disease-elimination claims from these performance results
- Do not disclose internal cost structures, unpublished trial data or confidential farm information
